# Chapter 1 — Purpose

This document records research directions that may influence Ledger in the future.

These are not commitments.

They are not roadmap items.

They are not guaranteed features.

Each topic represents an area worthy of investigation because it aligns with
Ledger's long-term vision as a Financial Intelligence Platform.

Ideas move from this document into formal specifications only after research,
validation, and architectural review.

===========================================================================

# Chapter 2 — Research Principles

Every research initiative should answer

What problem exists?

Why does it matter?

Does it align with Financial Truth?

Does it strengthen user understanding?

Can it remain explainable?

Can it preserve privacy?

Will it simplify financial management?

Research exists to discover opportunities.

Not to justify predetermined solutions.
